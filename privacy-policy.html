Privacy Policy for Driver Toolbox

Last updated: February 6, 2026

Aroniox (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the Driver Toolbox mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We do not collect or require any personal information such as name, email address, or account details. The App does not include user accounts or login functionality.

1.2 Location and Driving Data

The App may access and process the following data solely to provide core functionality:

  • Precise location (GPS)
  • Approximate location
  • Speed
  • Acceleration
  • Device sensor data related to driving

This data is:

  • Processed locally on your device
  • Not transmitted to our servers
  • Not shared with third parties

1.3 Camera Access

The App may request access to the device camera for specific features. Camera data is used only within the App and is not stored or transmitted externally.

2. Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

AdMob may collect and process certain information automatically, such as:

  • Device identifiers
  • Approximate location
  • Ad interaction data

This data is collected directly by Google in accordance with their privacy policy. We do not control or access the data collected by AdMob.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Data Storage

  • All driving-related and app-generated data is stored locally on your device
  • We do not operate any backend servers
  • We do not upload or synchronize data externally

You can remove all stored data by clearing the app data or uninstalling the App.

4. Data Sharing

We do not:

  • Sell user data
  • Share user data with third parties
  • Transfer data to external servers

The only third-party service used is Google AdMob, solely for displaying ads.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Legal Basis and Compliance

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with applicable data protection laws, including GDPR and similar regulations, by:

  • Minimizing data collection
  • Processing data locally
  • Providing transparency about third-party services

7. Future Features

The App may introduce new features in the future, such as additional tools or optional premium functionality. Any changes affecting data usage will be reflected in an updated Privacy Policy.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be effective when published. Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.
